A Bit About Us

Welcome to JoJo’s Creamery, a family-owned ice cream shop built on love, laughter, and sweet memories.

Founded by a mom, dad, and daughter in the heart of Willoughby, JoJo’s was created to bring people together. Inspired by family traditions and summer nights spent sharing ice cream, our shop is all about creating a warm, welcoming place where everyone feels at home.

From handcrafted flavors to friendly smiles, every scoop is made to be shared with the people you love most. 🍦

"Some of my favorite memories are the simple moments spent sharing ice cream with family, and that's exactly what I wanted to bring to JoJo's. Seeing people gather here, laugh together, and make their memories of their own means everything to me!"

Tina Lallo

Owner

"To me, JoJo's is more than just an ice cream shop; it's a family - a place where the community can come together. Whether you stop in for a quick pick-me-up or to celebrate a special occasion, we want everyone to feel welcome the second they walk through our doors!"

Jason Lallo

Owner

"I wanted JoJo's to feel fun, warm, and full of life. It is amazing to see people enjoy the flavors we created and turn our little dream into part of their own family memories!"

JoJo Lallo
